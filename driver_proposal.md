# Driver Proposal

| Driver | General Definition | Individual Perspective | Communal Perspective | Domain | Maslow's Level (Interpersonal) | Maslow's Level (Institutions/Society) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Trust** | Trust that institutions and community members act reliably and in the common interest. | How much I trust in institutions and people around me to act in my and the community's interest. | How much the community trusts institutions and each other. | Civic life | Love and belonging | Safety |
| **Security** | A sense of physical, psychological, and economic safety, free from threats or violence. | How safe I feel physically, financially, and psychologically in daily life; free from violence, intimidation, and instability. | The extent to which the community is free from crime, hazards, and economic shocks; presence of protective systems, preparedness, and social safety nets. | Foundations of well-being | Safety | Safety |
| **Self-esteem** | Feeling valued, confident, and capable as an individual. | My sense of worth, confidence, dignity, and ability to influence my own life. | The degree to which community norms, narratives, and institutions affirm the dignity and contributions of all members. | Personal empowerment and dignity | Love and belonging | Esteem |
| **Social support** | Access to practical and emotional help from family, friends, and the community. | Availability of practical help, advice, and emotional support from family, friends, neighbors, and colleagues when needed. | Strength, accessibility, and reciprocity of support networks and services (e.g., community centers, volunteering, mutual aid). | Social relationships and connections | Love and belonging | Love and belonging |
| **Empathy** | Experiencing and showing understanding, care, and compassion within the community. | How much I feel understood, respected, and cared for—and how I practice empathy toward others. | Prevailing culture of compassion and perspective-taking in public life, institutions, and everyday interactions. | Social relationships and connections | Love and belonging | Love and belonging |
| **Physical needs** | Reliable access to essentials such as clean air, food, water, shelter, clothing, and rest. | Whether my basic needs—clean air, nutritious food, safe water, shelter, clothing, and rest—are reliably met. | Infrastructure, policies, and services that ensure universal access to essentials (housing, clean water, sanitation, food security). | Foundations of well-being | Physiological needs | Physiological needs |
| **Physical health** | Energy, mobility, and the physical ability to function well in daily life. | My physical functioning, fitness, and freedom from disease or pain; ability to perform daily activities. | Population health status and health-promoting environments (e.g., safe streets, active transport, green spaces, access to care). | Foundations of well-being | Safety | Safety |
| **Mental health** | Emotional balance, resilience, and cognitive functioning such as focus and stress management. | My emotional well-being, resilience, stress levels, and cognitive functioning (attention, memory, concentration). | Prevalence of psychological well-being and availability, accessibility, and cultural acceptance of mental health support. | Foundations of well-being | Safety | Safety |
| **Meaning and Coherence** | A sense of purpose, direction, and understanding of one's life context. | Clarity of purpose, sense that life is meaningful, and that experiences fit together in a coherent way. | Shared narratives, cultural life, and institutions that provide orientation, continuity, and a sense that the city "makes sense." | Personal empowerment and dignity | Self-actualisation | Self-actualisation |
| **Participation and Inclusion** | Being able to take part in public life and feeling included in community spaces and processes. | My ability and opportunity to be heard, to participate in decisions, and to access public spaces and services without barriers. | Openness and fairness of civic processes and spaces; diversity and representation in decision-making and public life. | Civic life | Love and belonging | Esteem |
| **Justice and Non-Discrimination** | Fair treatment and equal opportunities, free from bias or exclusion. | Whether I am treated fairly regardless of identity or status and have equal access to rights, protections, and opportunities. | Existence, enforcement, and lived reality of fair rules and remedies; low disparities and anti-discrimination norms in institutions and workplaces. | Personal empowerment and dignity | Esteem | Safety |
| **Belonging and Identity** | Feeling connected to others with shared values, and identifying with the community. | Feeling accepted, seen, and connected to place, culture, and communities; pride in who I am. | Strength of social cohesion and inclusive identities that bridge differences across groups and neighborhoods. | Social relationships and connections | Love and belonging | Love and belonging |
